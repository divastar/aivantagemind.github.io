Example lesson format

Every lesson has a gentle rhythm.

  1. Name the idea.Start with one sentence and no jargon.
  2. Show a small example.Use coins, games, stories, diagrams, or everyday choices.
  3. Build the rule.Connect the example to a general pattern.
  4. Practice together.End with a question that invites thinking.
